How to read the numbers

  • Token counts are estimates: about 4 characters per token. Real counts differ by AI and by language, often by 10 to 30 percent.
  • Only text is counted. Images, files, web searches and the AI's hidden thinking are not in the exports, so real usage is higher.
  • Gemini exports have no chats, only prompts per day, so each day counts as one chat.

Part of the same family as chatvault, chatzip and chatrelay.
